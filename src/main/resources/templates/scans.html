The regulation

Ontario Regulation 191/11 — Integrated Accessibility Standards

Under the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005, Section 14 of O. Reg. 191/11 sets the legal bar for websites:

“Designated public sector organizations and large organizations shall make their internet websites and web content conform with the World Wide Web Consortium Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0, initially at Level A and increasing to Level AA.”
— O. Reg. 191/11, s. 14
Jan 1, 2014New sites: Level A Jan 1, 2021All sites: Level AA (current standard)
Read O. Reg. 191/11 WCAG 2.0 AA reference
Who has to comply, and what counts as an exception?

The Level AA requirement applies to public sector organizations of any size, and to private-sector or non-profit organizations with 50+ employees in Ontario, for web content posted after January 1, 2012. Live captions (WCAG success criterion 1.2.4) and pre-recorded audio description (1.2.5) are the only criteria explicitly excluded. The requirement can be waived only where meeting it is genuinely not practicable.

This is a plain-language summary for context, not legal advice — always confirm current obligations against the official regulation.

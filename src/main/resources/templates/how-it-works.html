Under the hood

From a URL to a fixed page

Here's exactly what happens between pasting in a link and knowing what to fix — start to finish, no steps skipped.

  1. 1

    Paste in a URL

    Drop any website's address into the scan box on the home page. No account needed — a guest scan works as a one-time view.

  2. 2

    The job gets queued

    Allyscan writes a row to Postgres with status QUEUED and hands the page back to you immediately — a full-site scan can take minutes, so nothing waits on it.

  3. 3

    A worker picks it up

    A background worker polls the queue every couple of seconds, claims the job, and marks it RUNNING.

  4. 4

    The site gets crawled

    A same-host crawler follows links breadth-first from the URL you gave it, discovering up to 20 pages to check.

  5. 5

    Every page gets scanned

    A real, headless Chromium browser opens each page and runs an axe-core accessibility scan against it — the same engine used across the industry for WCAG 2.0 AA / AODA checks.

  6. 6

    Violations are recorded

    Every failing rule is saved per page, tagged by category (ARIA, color contrast, forms, keyboard access, and more) and by impact — critical, serious, moderate, or minor.

  7. 7

    Claude writes a plain-language summary

    The full violation list is handed to the Claude API, which prioritizes the issues and explains them in plain language — a value-add on top of the raw report, not a dependency for it.

  8. 8

    You open the report

    Once the job flips to DONE, the report shows every violation grouped by page, filterable by category, alongside the AI summary.

  9. 9

    Click through to fix it

    Each violation's rule name links straight to that rule's page on Deque University — axe-core's own documentation — with a plain-English explanation of the problem and exactly how to fix it in your markup.
